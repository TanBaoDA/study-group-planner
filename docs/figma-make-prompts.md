# Prompts used in Figma Make (file `testHAIP`)

Prompts sent to the Figma Make AI on 26 Nov 2026, in order. Kept for the D3 "AI Use" section and so others can reproduce or adapt the changes. Earlier prompts that produced the first version of the prototype are in the Make file's chat history.

## 1. Diagonal (rectangle) drag selection

> In src/App.tsx, AvailabilityCalendar: make drag-select work diagonally, selecting a RECTANGLE across dates and times like when2meet, instead of only within one date column. Keep all other UI and behaviour unchanged. Details: (1) applyDrag(date, slot) should no longer require state.date === date. Compute from = dates.indexOf(state.date), to = dates.indexOf(date), lo = min(state.start, slot), hi = max(state.start, slot); start from a copy of state.original and, for every date in dates.slice(min(from,to), max(from,to)+1), add (if state.selecting) or delete (otherwise) slots lo..hi from that date's original set, then call onChange once with the whole object. Skip work if the same date|slot as the last call (store last on the drag ref, reset to "" on pointer down). (2) Replace per-cell onPointerEnter with an onPointerMove handler on the .time-grid div (only when not readOnly): if a drag is active, use document.elementFromPoint(e.clientX, e.clientY)?.closest("[data-date][data-slot]") and call applyDrag with its data-date and Number(data-slot). Add data-date={date} and data-slot={slot} to each cell button, and in the cell onPointerDown call e.currentTarget.releasePointerCapture?.(e.pointerId) after preventDefault so touch/pen drags also move across cells. Remove the now unused onPointerEnter prop from CalendarRow. (3) Change the hint "Click or drag within a date to select" to "Click or drag across dates and times to select", and "Click or drag within a date to select 30-minute blocks." to "Click or drag, also diagonally across dates, to select 30-minute blocks." Expected: pressing on Tue 09:00 and dragging to Thu 10:30 selects every cell Tue to Thu, 09:00 to 11:00; starting the drag on an already selected cell erases that rectangle instead.

## 2. Black-and-white minimalist restyle

> Restyle the whole app into the simplest possible minimalist black-and-white clickable prototype (like a clean wireframe). Keep ALL existing functionality, views, steps, state and logic exactly as they are (create event, share event, availability with click and diagonal rectangle drag-select, results, organiser view, prototype examples). Only change the visual styling. Rules: (1) Colours: only black (#000), white (#fff) and greys for borders and muted text. No purple, green, yellow, gradients, tinted backgrounds or coloured shadows anywhere. Page background plain white. (2) Remove decoration: no doodles, illustrations, pencil/sparkle drawings, sticky-note tape, emoji, dotted page background, heavy shadows or rounded pill shapes. Keep only functional icons (arrows, check, calendar) in black, small. (3) Typography: one system sans-serif font (system-ui), black text, simple sizes; headings bold, no display fonts. (4) Buttons: plain rectangles with 1px black border and small radius (4px). Primary button = black background with white text; secondary = white background with black text and black border. Hover: slightly grey background. Disabled: grey border and grey text. (5) Inputs, cards and panels: white with 1px light-grey or black border, no fills, minimal padding, no shadows. Step tabs at the top: simple bordered buttons, the active one black with white text. (6) Availability grid: white cells with thin grey grid lines; selected cells solid black (text inside white); hover a light grey. Results heatmap/matches: use shades of grey from light to black instead of colours, and best match marked with a black border or bold text. (7) Keep it accessible: text contrast at least 4.5:1, visible black focus outline on keyboard focus. Make the change mainly in src/index.css and remove decorative elements in JSX, without breaking any interaction. After the change, verify the app still builds, the 10 planner tests pass, and that every view is still clickable.

## 3. Standard mid-fidelity wireframe

> Now turn this into a standard MID-FIDELITY clickable wireframe, following common mid-fi conventions. Keep ALL functionality, views, state, logic, copy and the click + diagonal rectangle drag-select exactly as they are; only change visuals. Rules: (1) Grayscale only: #000, #333, #666, #999, #ccc, #e5e5e5, #f5f5f5, #fff. Use grey levels for hierarchy (e.g. #f5f5f5 for section backgrounds and table headers, #666 for secondary text), never any hue. (2) One font everywhere: a plain sans-serif (Inter or system-ui), max 3 text sizes plus bold for headings. Real text stays, no lorem ipsum. (3) Replace the logo and any image, avatar or illustration with a standard placeholder: a light grey box with a thin grey border and a diagonal X across it, same size as the original element. Keep simple functional icons (arrows, check, calendar, plus/minus) as thin black line icons. (4) Standard-looking components: rectangular buttons 36-40px high with 4px radius (primary = black fill + white text, secondary = white + 1px black border, tertiary = text link underlined); text inputs with 1px #999 border and a label above; stepper/tabs as a simple segmented bar with the active step black; lists and tables with thin #e5e5e5 dividers. (5) Layout: keep the current structure but align to an 8px spacing grid, consistent padding (16/24px), consistent card borders (1px #ccc, no shadows), left-aligned headings. (6) Availability grid: white cells, #e5e5e5 grid lines, selected = solid #333 with white text, hover = #f5f5f5; results heatmap in grey steps from #f5f5f5 to #000. (7) Remove anything purely decorative that remains (tape, sparkles, dotted backgrounds, tinted badges). Badges/tags become small outlined grey rectangles. (8) Keep focus outlines visible and contrast at least 4.5:1. Verify build, TypeScript and the 10 planner tests still pass and every view is still clickable.

## 4. 15-minute steps and text-only individual results

> Two changes. Keep the current mid-fi grayscale style and all other behaviour (including click and diagonal rectangle drag-select). (A) 15-minute granularity everywhere: change the time unit from 30 to 15 minutes. Session duration stepper: minimum 15 minutes, each minus/plus press changes it by exactly 15, always a multiple of 15 (label still shows e.g. "45 minutes · 45 min"). Daily survey window start/end pickers move in 15-minute steps (update the hint to "24-hour time · 15-minute steps"). Availability grid: one row per 15 minutes (slot = 15 min, time labels on the full hour and half hour are enough to keep it readable, quarter rows can have lighter grid lines), so the shortest selection is a single 15-minute cell. Update planner.ts (slot size, slotsBetween, countSlots, findMatches and any 30 constants), the sample/synthetic responses and the instruction texts ("30-minute blocks" becomes "15-minute blocks"), and update planner tests so they all pass with 15-minute slots. Keep the grid height reasonable (rows about half the old height) so the default window still fits with scrolling. (B) Results view: for other individuals' availability, do NOT show a grid/table/calendar per person anymore, it is hard to read and takes too much space. Instead show, for each respondent, their name followed by a compact plain-text list of free time ranges grouped by date, merging consecutive slots into ranges, e.g. "Mon 21 Sep: 09:00–11:15, 14:00–15:00" and "Tue 22 Sep: 10:00–12:00". Dates with no availability are omitted; if a person has none, show "No availability". Keep the existing overview/best-match results as they are. Verify build, TypeScript and all tests pass and every view is clickable.
